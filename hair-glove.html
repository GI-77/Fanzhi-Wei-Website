Hair Glove

Hair Glove explores the fragile boundary between the body and its material extensions. Light, delicate and vulnerable, hair is woven into a glove that simultaneously constrains, constructs and replaces the skin. The works question whether a material can become part of the body, while reflecting on intimacy, vulnerability and the limits of bodily presence.
